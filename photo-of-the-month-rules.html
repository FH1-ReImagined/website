Community · Photo of the Month

Rules

The rules of the Photo of the Month on forza.dyotson.dev. See this month's standings.

Prize

To be announced. The prize has not been decided yet. This section will say what the winner gets, and who can receive it, before the first contest starts.

Every winner's photo is featured on the home page of this website for the next month, with the photographer's gamertag, and stays in the past winners list.

Free to enter, free to vote

  • Entering and voting are free. No purchase, payment or donation is needed for either.
  • Donating never buys votes or entries, and never helps a photo win.
  • Votes cannot be bought, sold or traded.

Dates

  • Each contest is one calendar month in UTC: from 00:00 UTC on the 1st to 00:00 UTC on the 1st of the next month. Voting closes when the month ends.
  • The first contest month is to be announced.
  • The winner is named once the maintainers have checked the votes, usually within a few days after the month ends.

Entries

  • Any photo you take in the game's photo mode and share from the game during the month enters that month, once it is approved and shown in the community photos. You do not need to sign up to enter.
  • Only your own photos, taken in the game. The gallery's rules apply: nothing offensive, and no personal details in titles.
  • You may enter several photos; each one is ranked on its own.
  • A photo that is hidden or removed from the gallery, or whose player is banned from the server, leaves the contest.

Votes

  • A vote is a like (the thumbs-up) on an entry, given during the month, from a website account linked to the game.
  • The account's game must have its likes counting in public (a game counts once it has a speed trap or another record on the public server, from any time, single player included; a suspended account or a banned game does not count).
  • Likes that are not votes still show on the photo; they just do not count for the contest.
  • One like per game per photo. You cannot vote for your own photo. A like you take back is no longer a vote.
  • Who liked what is never shown. Only the totals are public.

Winning and ties

  • The entry with the most votes when the month ends wins.
  • Tie-break: the entry that reached its final number of votes first wins. If that is the same moment too, the photo uploaded first wins.
  • Until a winner is confirmed, the standings shown are provisional.

Checks and disqualification

  • The maintainers check the votes before naming the winner. Likes from vote farms, scripts, several accounts or games run by one person, or votes traded or paid for, are removed.
  • The maintainers may disqualify an entry or a player for vote manipulation, for breaking these rules or the gallery's rules, or for a photo that is not the player's own. Their decision is final.
  • A confirmed winner can still be withdrawn if manipulation is found later.
  • To report a problem, open an issue on the website's repository or ask on the Discord.

Other

  • The event is run by the FH1-ReImagined maintainers. It is not run, sponsored or endorsed by Microsoft, Playground Games or Turn 10 Studios.
  • By entering, you let this website show your photo, its title and your gamertag on the standings, the past winners list and the home page. To have a photo taken out of the past winners list, ask as above.
  • These rules may change between months; a month's contest follows the rules shown when it started.
