Designs

Paint jobs and vinyl groups players shared from the game's storefront, newest first. Each has a share code: search for it in the storefront and the design is yours, free.

How to get it in the game

  1. Open the Storefront and choose Search.
  2. Put the share code in the gamertag field and search.
  3. Pick the paint job and Buy it. It is free.
  4. Apply it to your car. A paint job fits the car it was made for.

      Rules

      • Only your own designs, made in the game.
      • Nothing offensive: no offensive text, logos or images, and no personal details in titles.
      • Shared designs are screened before they appear here. In the game, anyone can report a design: a design reported by three players is hidden until a maintainer checks it. The page refreshes every few minutes.
      • To remove one of your designs, choose REMOVE in MY SHARED DESIGNS in the game, or delete it on your account page with an account linked to your game. To have a design removed that breaks these rules, open an issue on the website's repository or ask on the Discord. Say which design: its share code.
