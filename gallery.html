    Rules

    • Only your own photos, taken in the game.
    • Nothing offensive: no offensive text, plates or liveries, and no personal details in titles.
    • Shared photos are screened automatically before they appear here. Anyone can report a photo: a reported photo is hidden until a maintainer checks it. The page refreshes every few minutes.
    • To delete one of your photos, sign in on your account page with an account linked to your game, and delete it there. To have a photo removed that breaks these rules, open an issue on the website's repository or ask on the Discord. Say which photo: its title and gamertag, or its address (open it and copy the image address).
