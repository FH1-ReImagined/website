    Your uploads

      Rules

      • Only your own work. Never the game's original files, and nothing you may not share.
      • A mod is a zip of a media folder with files of the game's own types at the game's paths, an optional mod.toml, and read-me or licence files (.txt, .md). A texture pack is a zip of <fingerprint>.dds or .png textures, an optional pack.toml, and .txt or .md files. The launcher's Add takes the same zips.
      • Refused: programs, scripts, archives inside the zip, links, paths that leave the zip's folder, files the game cannot load, and zips that unpack to more than 4 GB or to far more than their size. The upload is at most 512 MB.
      • Checked: every upload is scanned for malware in a separate, isolated checker and repacked with only its checked files; then a maintainer looks at it before it is listed.
      • Report a mod or pack that is broken, harmful, offensive or not the uploader's own: the report button is on its page. A report of malware from a player whose likes count hides it at once until a maintainer checks it.
      • To have something taken down (it is yours and was shared without your permission, or it holds the game's files), open an issue on the website's repository or ask on the Discord, with its address. You can delete your own uploads here.
