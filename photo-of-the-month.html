Photo of the Month

Every month, the shared photo with the most votes wins and is featured on the home page. A vote is a like from a website account linked to the game. The rules. Entering and voting are free.

    How to enter and vote

    • Enter: share a photo from photo mode in the game during the month. Every approved photo uploaded that month is in, with no sign-up.
    • Vote: sign in on your account page, link it to your game (upload one speed trap to the public server from the game, single player is fine, then in the launcher, with the main public server picked, Online > Online > Website account, or Settings > Online before launcher 0.2.9; the steps), then press the thumbs-up on the photos you like. You can like as many photos as you want, once each.
    • Which likes are votes: likes given during the month, from accounts that were at least 14 days old when they liked and whose game's likes count in public. Other likes still show on the photo, but they are not votes. Nobody can vote for their own photo.
    • Ties go to the photo that reached its number of votes first. The maintainers check the votes before naming the winner. All the rules.
