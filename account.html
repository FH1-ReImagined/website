Sign in

Sign in with Discord, GitHub or an email address. Signing in the first time makes your account; there is no separate sign-up.

How it works

  1. Sign in here with Discord, GitHub or email.
  2. In the launcher, with the main public server picked: Online > Online > Website account > Link (before launcher 0.2.9: Settings > Online > Website account).
  3. Click Link here, then confirm in the launcher.

Your game needs one speed trap or record on the public server first, from any time (single player is fine; the steps). Photos come from the game's photo mode and designs from the in-game storefront: the website shows them, with their likes, but they are not uploaded here.

It works for 10 minutes, in this browser only. No mail? Check your spam folder, or ask for a new code.

You must be 13 or older to make an account. From Discord or GitHub the website keeps only your account's number there, not your name or email address. An email address is never stored: only a one-way hash of it. Details: the privacy policy.
