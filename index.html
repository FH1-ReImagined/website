Online again

Back online

The official online services closed on 22 August 2023 (Turn 10's notice). The public server brings them back: Horizon World is the first playlist under Multiplayer, Find Game.

  • Shared free roam for up to 8 players per world
  • Leaderboards and Rivals, friends and chat
  • A co-op festival to race with friends
  • The public server, or one you host
