Forza Horizon 1ReImagined

The original 2012 Forza Horizon, recompiled to run natively on Windows and Linux, and online again.

The official online services closed on 22 August 2023 (Turn 10's notice). The public server has Horizon World, shared free roam for up to 8 players, with leaderboards and Rivals. You can host your own.
